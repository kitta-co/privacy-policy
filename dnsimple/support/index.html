Using DNSimple

Choose a DNS resolver or DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint, then tap Connect and approve the iOS VPN configuration. The VPN is a local packet tunnel used only to route DNS requests to the resolver you selected; DNSimple is not a remote VPN service.

Privacy

DNSimple has no accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, or Kitta Co backend services. Kitta Co does not receive DNS requests, usage data, local DNS logs, custom host overrides, or settings. Optional logs and statistics remain on your device. DNS queries are sent only to the resolver you choose.
