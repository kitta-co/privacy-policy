Privacy Policy

Telisten

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Telisten handles information when you use it. Telisten is published by Kitta Co.

Telisten does not send your Telegram credentials, messages, media, or listening history to Kitta Co. The app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or Kitta Co backend service.

Telegram Account

Telisten connects directly from your device to Telegram using Telegram's MTProto protocol. Your phone number, login codes, account information, chats, messages, media, reactions, replies, forwards, and playlist channels are processed by Telegram when you use the related features. Telegram handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy. Telisten does not use a bot, proxy, or Kitta Co server for this connection.

Lyrics

When lyrics are requested, Telisten sends the track title, artist, and duration to LRCLIB to find a match. It does not send the audio file, your phone number, or your Telegram login credentials to LRCLIB. Returned lyrics are cached on your device.

Data on Your Device

Telegram authorization keys are stored in the system Keychain. Account labels, app preferences, the local music-chat index, favorites, downloaded audio, artwork, chat avatars, and lyrics are stored locally on your device. Telisten may automatically remove older cached files when its cache limit is reached. This local data is not uploaded to Kitta Co.

Your Controls

You can remove downloaded tracks in Telisten. Signing out removes the selected account's Telisten authorization keys from the Keychain and asks Telegram to end that session. Deleting the app removes its local app data through iOS or macOS. Reactions, comments, forwarded playlist entries, and playlist channels are Telegram content and can also be managed through Telegram.

Platform and Distribution

Apple, Telegram, and LRCLIB may process technical, account, or network information under their own policies when you download, authenticate, or use their services. That processing is not governed by this policy.

Changes

If Telisten's data practices change materially in a future version, we will update this page and, where required, provide notice through the app or its store listing.
